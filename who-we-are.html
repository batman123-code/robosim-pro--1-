WHO WE ARE

Act For Change Foundation

COMPASSION • EDUCATION • EMPOWERMENT • SUSTAINABLE IMPACT

Act For Change Foundation is dedicated to creating lasting social impact through education, community development, environmental initiatives, and humanitarian action. Together, we empower individuals, strengthen communities, and build a more compassionate future.

Learn Our Story Explore Programs
Compassion, Education, and Empowerment

A Story That Begins With Compassion and Grows Into Impact

Since our inception, Act For Change has stood as a beacon of hope for marginalised communities. We believe that real change starts at the grassroots level — where compassion meets action.

Operating out of Kolkata, our dedicated team of volunteers and professionals work tirelessly to bridge the gap between privilege and poverty. We don't just provide aid; we build sustainable pathways out of poverty through education, healthcare, and livelihood support.

Every child in school, every woman running a business, and every village with clean water represents our ongoing commitment to humanity. We are not just an NGO; we are a movement for a brighter, more equitable India.

Our True North

Our Vision for Change

Our vision extends beyond temporary relief. We envision an India where education, empowerment, and environmental sustainability intersect to transform entire communities for generations to come.

Vision & Mission

Our blueprint for a resilient, self-sustaining society.

Our Mission
