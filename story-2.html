Rahul's Journey

Rural Bengal

Rahul was forced to drop out of school at age 10 to help his family make ends meet. Today, he's a confident youth leader driving change in his village.

Their Struggle

The loss of his father meant Rahul had to become the breadwinner. Educational aspirations were replaced with long hours of hard labor. The lack of guidance and financial instability stripped him of his childhood.

The Turning Point

The foundation's child welfare team intervened, providing his family with a monthly stipend so Rahul could return to school. He was paired with a dedicated mentor who helped him catch up on years of lost education.

"Education didn't just give me knowledge; it gave me the courage to dream again."

The Transformation

Not only did Rahul excel academically, but he also discovered a passion for community service. He now runs a weekend tutoring program for other underprivileged kids in his village.
