Act For Change — A Trusted NGO in Kolkata Creating Real Change in India

We are dedicated to building a brighter future. Through sustainable initiatives and community-driven action, Act For Change focuses on empowering individuals across West Bengal and India to overcome poverty, find educational opportunities, and rebuild their lives with dignity.

Our Missions

Society

Uplifting communities through education, healthcare, and resource access.

Environment

Driving sustainable practices, tree planting, and climate awareness programs.

Change

Empowering individuals to break the cycle of poverty and lead independent lives.

Our Key Activities

Environmental Programs

Promoting sustainability, afforestation, and clean water drives.

Education Camps

Providing foundational learning and school support for underprivileged kids.

Books & Learning Drives

Bridging the knowledge gap through community libraries.

Women Awareness

Focused programs empowering women through skills and health awareness.

Child Welfare

Ensuring every child is safe, nourished, and free from labor.

Community Development

Building local infrastructure and strengthening rural economies.

Disaster Relief

Emergency response, food distribution, and rebuilding efforts.

Healthcare Support

Medical camps, hygiene campaigns, and essential health supplies.
